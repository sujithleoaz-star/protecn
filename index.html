About PRO TEC

Chennai’s entrance automation specialist

PRO TEC Entrance Automation is an engineering firm based in Ayappakkam, Chennai. We manufacture, supply and install automatic gates, barriers and doors for factories, warehouses, IT corridors, apartments and luxury villas across Tamil Nadu.

Every system pairs a reliable motor, remote controls, photocells and safety sensors with heavy-duty structural engineering, backed by genuine spares and a local service team that answers breakdown calls fast.
