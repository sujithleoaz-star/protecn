Overhead Sectional Doors

Overhead sectional doors lift upwards and rest flat under the ceiling, saving space inside and outside your building. PRO TEC supplies motorised sectional doors for homes, workshops, showrooms and warehouses.

  • Space-saving vertical lift
  • Motorised operation with remote
  • Plain or glazed panel designs

Overview

A sectional door is made of horizontal panels that move along side tracks. Because it lifts straight up, it needs no clear space in front of the opening, so vehicles can park right up to it.

We help you pick the panel design and finish, install the tracks and motor accurately, and set up the remote and safety devices. Service and spares are available from our local team.
