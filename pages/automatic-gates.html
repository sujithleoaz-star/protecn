Overview

An automatic gate saves you from getting out of the vehicle to open and close the gate, and it keeps the entrance closed and secure the rest of the time. The right type depends on your space, gate weight and how many vehicles use it each day.

PRO TEC helps you choose between sliding and swing gates, matches the motor to the gate, and handles the installation, wiring, sensors and testing. We also provide spares and annual maintenance.
