Overview

A rolling shutter coils into a compact box above the opening and gives strong security when closed. A motor lifts and lowers it, so even large shutters are effortless to operate.

Aluminium shutters resist corrosion and wear and come in several colours, while GI (galvanised iron) shutters give strong protection for shops, godowns and industrial units. We measure, supply, install and service both.
